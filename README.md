# Ken and Flo Jacobs Collection

## Description
Ken Jacobs is an experimental filmmaker from Brooklyn New York. Since the 1950s, he has made over 150 films with his wife, Flo Jacobs. Jacobs was founder of the nonprofit filmmakers' co-operative Millennium Film Workshop in New York city and co-founder of the SUNY system's first Department of Cinema, at Binghamton University. His honors include the American Film Institute's Maya Deren Award (1994), a Guggenheim Award (1995), and the Stan Brakeage Vision Award (2004), among others. 

The collection contains materials related to the filmmaking, creative work, academic work, and teaching of Ken and Flo Jacobs, including family and biographical material of relevance to their creative work and personal material relating to their relationships with other artists, filmmakers, scholars, and other figures. The papers include personal records and correspondence, filmmaking records, teaching materials, rare and unique books or publications, and sound and video recordings.
